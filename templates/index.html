        Quick Farming Tips

        Monitor Soil Moisture

        Check sensor data regularly to maintain optimal soil moisture levels (40-60%). Prevent waterlogging and drought stress.

        Sunlight Exposure

        Ensure 6-8 hours of direct sunlight daily for optimal growth and fruit development.

        Ideal Temperature

        Maintain temperature between 20-30°C for best results. Protect from frost and extreme heat.

        Soil Requirements

        Use well-draining soil with pH 4.5-6.5. Add organic matter to improve fertility.

        Pest Control

        Inspect plants weekly for mealybugs and scale insects. Use organic pesticides when needed.

        Harvest Time

        Harvest when fruit turns golden-yellow and emits sweet aroma (18-22 months after planting).

        Growth Monitoring Guide

        1-6m

        Vegetative Stage

        Focus on leaf development. Expect 30-40 leaves. High nitrogen fertilizer needed.

        Monitor: Leaf color, root establishment
        7-12m

        Pre-Flowering

        Plant prepares for flowering. Balanced fertilization. Watch for flower initiation signs.

        Monitor: Plant vigor, crown formation
        13-18m

        Flowering & Fruiting

        Fruit develops and matures. High potassium needed. Regular disease checks critical.

        Monitor: Fruit size, color change, pest activity
        18-22m

        Harvest Ready

        Fruit fully mature with golden color and sweet aroma. Handle carefully during harvest.

        Monitor: Color, fragrance, firmness
