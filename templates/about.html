About PiñaSense

Advanced AI-powered pineapple disease detection system for modern farmers

Our Mission

PiñaSense was built to help pineapple farmers detect crop diseases quickly and accurately. Through artificial intelligence and advanced image processing, we help identify problems early before they become severe, enabling farmers to take preventive action.

How It Works

The system uses advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze pineapple leaf and fruit images. It identifies patterns and color signatures associated with different diseases with high accuracy.

  • Image/video upload and preprocessing
  • AI-powered disease detection (98% accuracy)
  • Real-time analysis and instant results
  • Personalized treatment recommendations
  • Complete detection history tracking

Detectable Diseases

Heart Rot

Caused by Phytophthora spp. - one of the most serious pineapple diseases.

Fruit Rot

Caused by Thielaviopsis paradoxa - causes dark discoloration and fruit damage.

Yellow Spot

Caused by Curvularia spp. - yellow leaf spots that may spread quickly.

Pink Disease

Caused by Pantoea citrea - pink internal discoloration affecting fruit.

Healthy

No disease detected - pineapple in excellent condition requiring regular maintenance.

How to Use AgriPinya
