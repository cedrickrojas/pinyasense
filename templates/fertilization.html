Fertilization by Growth Stage

Pineapple nutrient requirements across different growth stages

Vegetative Stage (1-6 months)

Focus: Leaf development and root growth

NPK Ratio: High Nitrogen (20-10-10)

Amount: 70g per plant

Frequency: Every 2 months

Goal: Large healthy leaves for stronger photosynthesis

Pre-Flowering (7-10 months)

Focus: Preparation for flowering

NPK Ratio: Balanced (14-14-14)

Amount: 60g per plant

Frequency: Every 6-8 weeks

Goal: Strengthen plant for flower/fruit production

Fruiting Stage (11-18 months)

Focus: Fruit development and quality

NPK Ratio: High Potassium (10-10-30)

Amount: 80g per plant

Frequency: Every 2 months

Goal: Large, sweet, high-quality fruits

Supplemental Feeding

Type: Foliar spray

NPK Ratio: 15-30-15 + micronutrients

Amount: 3g per liter water

Frequency: Bi-weekly

Timing: Early morning application

Application Methods

Side Dressing (Recommended)

  • Apply fertilizer 15-20cm away from plant base
  • Form circular band around the plant
  • Mix lightly with top 2-3cm of soil
  • Water thoroughly after application
  • Best for granular fertilizers

Foliar Application

  • Dilute as per manufacturer's instructions
  • Spray on both sides of leaves (especially undersides)
  • Apply early morning (6-8 AM) or late afternoon (4-6 PM)
  • Avoid application if rain expected within 6 hours
  • Quick nutrient uptake, good for deficiencies

Broadcast Application

  • Scatter evenly over soil surface
  • Incorporate lightly with soil
  • Water immediately to prevent nutrient loss
  • Good for large areas but less efficient

Essential Micronutrients

Micronutrients are needed in small amounts but are essential for healthy growth

Iron (Fe)

Function: Chlorophyll production

Deficiency: Young leaves turn yellow (veins stay green)

Application: Chelated iron foliar spray

Zinc (Zn)

Function: Enzyme activation, hormone production

Deficiency: Small narrow leaves, stunted growth

Application: Zinc sulfate spray or soil application

Manganese (Mn)

Function: Photosynthesis, enzyme systems

Deficiency: Yellowing between leaf veins

Application: Manganese sulfate foliar spray

Boron (B)

Function: Cell wall formation, fruit development

Deficiency: Brittle leaves, poor fruit quality

Application: Borax soil application (careful - toxic in excess)

Organic Fertilizer Options

Compost

Amount: 2-3 kg per plant

Frequency: Every 3 months

Benefits: Improves soil structure, water retention, microbial activity

NPK: Variable (typically 1-1-1)

Vermicast

Amount: 1 kg per plant

Frequency: Monthly

Benefits: Rich in beneficial microorganisms and enzymes

NPK: Approximately 2-1-1

Chicken Manure

Amount: 500g per plant (composted only!)

Frequency: Every 2-3 months

Benefits: High nitrogen, slow release

NPK: Approximately 3-2-1

Seaweed Extract

Amount: 50ml per liter (diluted 1:20)

Frequency: Bi-weekly foliar spray

Benefits: Growth hormones, trace minerals, stress tolerance

Application: Foliar and soil drench

Important Warnings & Best Practices

  • Do not over-fertilize! Over-fertilization is often more harmful than under-fertilization.
  • Do not apply on dry soil - Irrigate first before fertilizing.
  • Avoid rain periods - Do not apply before rain or storms.
  • Apply at the right time - Best time: early morning (6-8 AM) or late afternoon (4-6 PM).
  • Keep records - Track each application for better planning.
  • Soil test - Perform a soil test every 6 months for accurate recommendations.
  • Observe plants - Watch for signs of deficiency or excess nutrients
  • Storage - Store fertilizers in cool, dry place away from sunlight
  • Safety - Wear gloves and mask when handling chemicals
  • Environment - Apply carefully to avoid runoff to waterways
